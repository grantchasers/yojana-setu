How Does MoSJE Interest Subvention Protect Your Livelihood?

Commercial moneylenders and non-institutional financiers often levy flat rates exceeding 24% to 36% p.a. Under the National Scheduled Castes & Backward Classes Finance Development Corporations (NSFDC / NBCFDC), the Central Government bridges the interest deficit directly to channel banks.

check_circle Simple Declining Balance

Interest is calculated exclusively on the outstanding principal balance, never on the original sanction amount. Every timely payment directly diminishes future interest dues.

check_circle Zero Foreclosure Penalties

Beneficiaries are empowered to settle loans ahead of schedule or make lump-sum payments during bumper agricultural or enterprise cycles without any punitive charges.

check_circle Quarterly Moratorium Audit

In cases of documented localized climate disturbances or raw-material market delays, community extension officers can recommend extending the principal grace period by up to 90 days.
